General League Rules

• All picks are against the spread

• Picks must be submitted before kickoff

• Missed picks count as losses

• Push counts as a correct pick (1 point)

• Spreads lock at kickoff — picks can be changed up until then

• Multi-segment winners keep all cash prizes; playoff entry passes to next qualified player

H2H + Points
Pick Format

• Pick all games against the spread

• Each correct pick = 1 point

Head-to-Head

• H2H win = 6 bonus points

• H2H tie = 3 points each

Scoring

• 16 games × 1 pt + H2H win (6 pts) = Max 22 pts/week

• Points accrue through end of Week 6

Segment Winner

• Highest total points across Weeks 1–6

Segment winner → auto playoff entry + $25
Tiebreakers

1. H2H record between tied players

2. Highest single-week score within Weeks 1–6

3. Most H2H wins across Weeks 1–6

H2H + Weighted Confidence
Pick Format

• Choose any 8 games against the spread

• Assign confidence: 8 (most) down to 1 (least)

• Correct pick earns its weighted value

Head-to-Head

• H2H win = 10 bonus points

• H2H tie = 5 points each

Scoring

• 36 max weighted + 10 H2H = Max 46 pts/week

• Points accrue through end of Week 12

Segment Winner

• Highest total weighted points across Weeks 7–12

Segment winner → auto playoff entry + $25
Tiebreakers

1. H2H record between tied players

2. Highest single-week score within Weeks 7–12

3. Most H2H wins across Weeks 7–12

Divisional Round-Robin
Pick Format

• Pick all games against the spread

Head-to-Head

• Most correct picks wins the matchup

• Tied → weekly tiebreaker decides

• Same tiebreaker → .5 W / .5 L each

Divisions

• 4 divisions of 4 players

• Face each division opponent twice over 6 weeks

• Standings determined by win-loss record

4 division winners → auto playoff entry + $10 each
Tiebreakers

1. H2H record vs. tied opponents

2. Most correct picks in H2H matchups vs. tied

3. Highest correct picks in any one week vs. tied

4. Most correct picks across Weeks 13–18

5. Best single-week score

Playoff Format — 8 Players
Auto-Qualifiers (6)

• Weeks 1–6 segment winner

• Weeks 7–12 segment winner

• 4 division winners from Weeks 13–18

Wild Card (2)

• Top 2 overall season point totals among non-qualified players

Wild Card Round

• All 8 players pick all games ATS

• Assign confidence weights 1–4

• Top 4 in weighted points advance

Final Rounds (4 Players)

• Carry Wild Card points forward

• Pick all remaining playoff games ATS

• Divisional: 2 pts per correct pick

• Championship: 4 pts per correct pick

• Super Bowl: 8 pts for correct pick

• Top 3 in total cumulative points receive payouts

1st: $200 · 2nd: $75 · 3rd: $35
Qualification Tiebreakers

1. H2H record (if applicable)

2. Best overall record Weeks 1–18

Wild Card Tiebreakers

1. Weekly tiebreaker

2. Total points scored in 2 AFC Wild Card games

3. Total points scored in 2 NFC Wild Card games

Finals Tiebreakers

1. Weekly tiebreaker for the Super Bowl

2. Closest guess to longest field goal made in Super Bowl
