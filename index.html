Weeks 1–6  H2H + Points

Pick Format

All games against the spread. Each correct pick earns 1 point.

Head-to-Head

Weekly H2H matchup win awards 6 bonus points. H2H tie awards 3 points to each player.

Scoring

Example: 16 games (1 pt each) + H2H win (6 pts) = Max 22 pts/week. Points earned weekly accrue through end of Week 6.

Segment Winner

Highest total points across Weeks 1–6.

Segment Winner receives auto playoff entry + $25 prize
Tiebreakers

1) H2H record between tied players (applies only if matched within Weeks 1–6)
2) Highest points scored in a single week within Weeks 1–6
3) Most H2H wins across Weeks 1–6

Weeks 7–12  H2H + Weighted

Pick Format

Choose any 8 games against the spread. Assign a confidence weight to each pick (8 = most confident down to 1 = least).

Head-to-Head

Weekly H2H win awards 10 points plus weighted points from each correct pick. H2H tie awards 5 points to each player.

Scoring

Points per week: 36 weighted + 10 H2H = Max 46 pts/week. Points earned weekly accrue through end of Week 12.

Segment Winner

Highest total weighted points across Weeks 7–12.

Segment Winner receives auto playoff entry + $25 prize
Tiebreakers

1) H2H record between tied players (applies only if matched within Weeks 7–12)
2) Highest points scored in a single week within Weeks 7–12
3) Most H2H wins across Weeks 7–12

Weeks 13–18  Divisions

Pick Format

All games against the spread.

Head-to-Head

Player with the most correct picks against opponent earns the win. If tied, the weekly tiebreaker determines the winner. If opponents share the same tiebreaker, each earns .5 win and .5 loss.

Scoring

Total Wins / Total Losses / Total Correct Picks (Weeks 13–18). H2H tie awards .5 to each player.

Divisions

4 divisions of 4. Each player faces divisional opponents twice over the 6-week segment. Standings determined by record.

Segment winners receive auto playoff entry + $10 prize
Tiebreakers

1) 3/4-way: most H2H wins vs. tied opponents · 2-way: H2H record
2) 3/4-way: most correct picks in all H2H vs. tied opponents · 2-way: most correct picks in 2 H2H matchups
3) 3/4-way: highest correct picks in any one week vs. tied opponents · 2-way: most correct picks in 1 week of H2H
4) Most correct picks in Weeks 13–18
5) Most correct picks in any single week

Playoffs — 8 Players

Qualification

6 auto-qualifiers: Weeks 1–6 winner, Weeks 7–12 winner, and 4 division winners from Weeks 13–18.

2 wild cards: Top 2 overall season point totals among players not already qualified.

Wild Card Round

All 8 players pick all games against the spread, weighted 1–4 confidence. Top 4 in weighted points advance.

Final Rounds

Advancing players carry their Wild Card points forward. All 4 pick against the spread through Divisional → Championship → Super Bowl.

Divisional: 2 pts/game   Championship: 4 pts/game   Super Bowl: 8 pts

Top 3 in total points receive payouts.

Playoff Tiebreakers

Qualification

1) H2H record (if applicable)
2) Best record Weeks 1–18

Wild Card Round

1) Weekly tiebreaker
2) Total points scored in 2 AFC Wild Card games
3) Total points scored in 2 NFC Wild Card games

Final Rounds

1) Weekly tiebreaker for the Super Bowl
2) Closest guess to the longest field goal made in the Super Bowl

Payouts

Weeks 1–6 Winner $25
Weeks 7–12 Winner $25
Division Winners (×4) $10 ea.
Playoff 1st $200 Playoff 2nd $75
Playoff 3rd $35

Standard Rules

▸ All picks are against the spread
▸ Picks must be submitted before kickoff
▸ Missed picks count as losses
▸ Push counts as a correct pick (1 point)
▸ Spreads lock at kickoff — picks can be changed up until kickoff
▸ If a player wins multiple segments, they keep all cash prizes; playoff entry passes to the next highest non-qualified player
